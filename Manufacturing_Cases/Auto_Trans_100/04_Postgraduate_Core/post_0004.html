curiosity
好奇心
 noun
名词
 (pl. curiosities)
（复数好奇心）
 1 [uncountable,singular] curiosity (about something)| curiosity (to do something) a strong desire to know
[不可数，单数] 好奇心（关于某事）| 好奇心（做某事）强烈的求知欲
 about something
关于某事
 Children show curiosity about everything.
孩子们对一切都表现出好奇。
 a certain curiosity to see what would happen next
好奇接下来会发生什么
 The letter wasn't addressed to me but I opened it out of curiosity.
这封信不是写给我的，但我出于好奇打开了它。
 His answer did not satisfy my curiosity at all.
他的回答根本不能满足我的好奇心。
 Sophie's curiosity was aroused by the mysterious phone call.
苏菲被这个神秘的电话勾起了好奇心。
 intellectual curiosity
求知欲
 ‘Why do you ask?’ ‘Oh, just idle curiosity ’ (= no particular reason).
“你为什么问？”“哦，只是无聊的好奇心”（ 没有特别的原因）。
 2 [countable] an unusual and interesting thing
[可数] 一件不寻常且有趣的事情
 The museum is full of historical curiosities.
博物馆充满了历史珍品。
 curiosity killed the cat (saying) used to tell somebody not to ask questions or try to find out
好奇害死猫（说）曾经告诉某人不要问问题或试图找出答案
 about things that do not concern them
关于与他们无关的事情
